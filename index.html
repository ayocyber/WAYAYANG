04 Field to table

One continuous chain, from the field to the customer.

Four stages, managed as one process — so quality isn’t lost between the farm and the people who depend on our product.

01/ 04
Illustrative · Harvest, Nigeria
  1. 01 Stage one

    Sourcing

    Raw agricultural produce is sourced directly, with quality checks beginning at the point of origin — so standards are set before anything leaves the field.

  2. 02 Stage two

    Processing

    Modern technology and trained hands turn raw produce into consistent, market-ready product, batch after batch.

  3. 03 Stage three

    Quality assurance

    Every batch is checked against our internal standard before it earns the Wyayang name.

  4. 04 Stage four

    Delivery

    Reliable distribution gets finished product to customers and stakeholders on schedule, as agreed.
